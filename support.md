# Skip For You – Support & FAQ

**Skip For You** lets you turn off the **“For You” feed** on X.com and Instagram when browsing in **Safari**.  

Take control of your social media experience and avoid distractions — all without tracking.  

---

## Features
- Turn off the **“For You” tab on X**  
- Turn off the **“For You” feed on Instagram**  
- Works **directly inside Safari**  
- **No tracking** — everything runs locally on your device  
- Runs **entirely on your device**  

---

## How to Enable
1. Open **Settings** on your iPhone or iPad  
2. Tap **Safari → Extensions**  
3. Enable **“Skip For You”**  
4. Allow access for **x.com** and **instagram.com**  

> Works **only in Safari**.  

---

## FAQ

**Q: Does Skip For You track my activity?**  
**A:** No. All functionality runs locally on your device. Your browsing data is **not collected or transmitted**.  

**Q: Can I use this extension on other browsers?**  
**A:** No. Currently, Skip For You only works in Safari.  

**Q: Does this app work on iPhone and iPad?**  
**A:** Yes, as long as you are using **Safari**.  

**Q: Can I block other tabs or feeds?**  
**A:** No. The extension only targets the **“For You” feed** on X.com and Instagram.  

**Q: Do I need an account to use this extension?**  
**A:** No. It works without any login or account.
